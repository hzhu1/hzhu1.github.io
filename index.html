Bio

I work on LLM infrastructure at ByteDance Seed, building and optimizing training frameworks for large-scale pre-training and post-training. I obtained my PhD degree from Johns Hopkins University in 2022, advised by Prof. Xin Jin. I received my bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University, China.

Education
